ATHRION Privacy Policy

DRAFT: review and complete this policy (company name, contact address, effective date) before publishing the app.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address.
  • Profile and goals: age, sex, height, weight, fitness goal, experience level and training preferences.
  • Training and nutrition logs: workouts, sets, weights, food entries, weekly check-ins, body measurements and progress photos you add.
  • Health data, only if you allow it: daily steps, sleep duration, resting heart rate and active energy from Apple Health or Health Connect.

How we use it

  • To build and adapt your training plan and nutrition targets.
  • To show your progress, records, streaks and badges.
  • To send workout reminders, if you turn them on.

Your profile, recent training, check-ins and health averages are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate your plan. Meal photos are sent to the same provider to estimate nutrition.

Health data

Health data read from Apple Health or Health Connect is used only to personalise your training plan and show your trends in the app. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties other than as described above. ATHRION does not write to Apple Health or Health Connect.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored in our database hosted by Supabase. Progress photos are stored privately and are only accessible to your account.

Your choices

  • You can revoke health access at any time in Apple Health or Health Connect settings.
  • You can delete entries, measurements and photos in the app.
  • To delete your account and all associated data, contact us.
